---
name: Researcher signup friction
description: Owner-directed separation of minimal researcher registration from wholesale application requirements.
---

Keep researcher registration distinct from wholesale applications. The owner asked
to remove excessive inputs and guardrails that discourage legitimate researchers.

**Why:** workplace-email wording, exact birth-date collection, research-setting
questions and wholesale application choices made ordinary researcher registration
look restricted to institutions.

**How to apply:** researcher registration uses explicit 21+ self-attestation and
research-use assent, not a claim that age has been independently verified. Keep
terms consent, email verification, password protection and optional marketing
consent distinct. Do not reintroduce wholesale approval or corporate-email
requirements into this path. Apply equivalent behavior to password and Google
signup; preserve existing wholesale and legacy-form safeguards.
