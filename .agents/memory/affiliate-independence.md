---
name: Affiliate independence
description: Why affiliate attribution and commission eligibility are separate decisions.
---

Treat a referral cookie as attribution only. Do not accrue commission unless a staff-reviewed record establishes that the referred buyer is independent of the affiliate.

**Why:** Account IDs and email addresses are attacker-controlled boundaries; an affiliate can create additional accounts and mailboxes. Automatically paying from those signals enables self-referral losses.

**How to apply:** Any future affiliate flow may improve the staff review mechanism, but must keep commission accrual fail-closed and preserve the reviewer and verification time as auditable data.
